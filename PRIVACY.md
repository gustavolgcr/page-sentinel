# Privacy policy — Page Sentinel

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

## Summary

Page Sentinel does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything stays in your browser.

## What the extension stores

Only in Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your computer:

- the addresses of the sites you asked it to watch and the interval for each one;
- the text of the latest version of each site, used to detect changes;
- the time of the last check and the lines that changed.

This data never leaves your computer and is deleted when you remove the site or uninstall the extension.

## Access to websites

The extension asks for access to all sites because you can choose to watch any address. It only loads and reads the sites **you** added, at the intervals **you** set. It does not read, record or modify the pages you browse normally.

## Third parties

There is no server, analytics, advertising or data sharing of any kind. The only connections made are to the sites you asked it to watch.
